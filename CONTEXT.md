# UZStock

UZStock presents Uzbekistan securities, trading activity and source-backed issuer analysis.

## Language

**Issuer**:
The organization that issues a security and publishes financial statements. One issuer can have several securities or share classes.
_Avoid_: Stock, instrument (when referring to the organization)

**Instrument**:
A specific tradable security, identified by its ticker and/or ISIN. Its market price and share basis are distinct from its issuer's financial results.

**Market session**:
The dated exchange trading session to which a quote or aggregate belongs. Collection time and the date of a negotiated deal do not establish a newer auction session.

**Negotiated deal**:
A separately reported negotiated trade. Its amount and quantity remain separate from auction-session totals.

**Financial snapshot**:
An issuer's financial figures for one reporting standard, period and scope, together with source references, comparable periods and data-quality limitations.

**Verified report**:
An analysis built from a financial snapshot whose published figures remain traceable to their source and period. Missing or blocked figures are not replaced with zero.
