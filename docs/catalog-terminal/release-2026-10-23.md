  June–July 2020 NPL, January–February 2020 structure. Published pages or their
  attachments are absent, unusable or for a different observation month.
  October 2026 monthly tables were not yet published at collection time.

  source values are never invented to satisfy a coverage target.

  require actual elapsed observation. They are **not accepted in advance**.
