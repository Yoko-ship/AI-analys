What this site does

A minimal front end for stock analysis in Uzbekistan. The site lets users register, sign in, pick a company, and get a structured analysis with score, grade, verdict, and report sections.

1. Register Create an account with email and password.
2. Choose company Pick from the available list or type a ticker.
3. Get analysis See score, verdict, metrics, and report sections.
